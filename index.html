PHOTO GUIDE

A better photo helps review

  • 01
    Use good light

    Bright, even light helps show the area clearly.

  • 02
    Hold steady and focus

    Keep the camera close enough to see details.

  • 03
    Show the whole area

    Avoid fingers, shadows, blur and unrelated objects.

  • 04
    Get consent first

    Explain the photograph and obtain patient consent.

No automatic diagnosis

Patient cannot hear?

If the microphone check stays flat, open this appointment's Jitsi link on a phone as the doctor's second device. Use the phone for microphone and sound, keep its camera off, and mute the laptop's Jitsi microphone and speakers to prevent echo. Confirm the patient hears a short phrase before consulting.
